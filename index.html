Welcome to Peniel

A Place Where God's Divine Presence is Made Real

Gaskiya Baptist Church (Sanctuary of Peniel) is a vibrant, Christ-centered fellowship established under the Kaduna Baptist Conference and affiliated with the Nigerian Baptist Convention.

Named after the biblical Peniel (Genesis 32:30), where Jacob declared, "For I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved", our church is a sanctuary dedicated to authentic encounters with God through fervent prayer, sound biblical preaching, Spirit-filled praise, and godly fellowship.

Sound Biblical Truth

Preaching the uncompromised word in season and out of season.

Real Presence of God

A place of divine encounter, spiritual growth, and restoration.

Nurturing Community

Vibrant ministries for men, women, youth, students, and children.

Spiritual Worship

From The Pastor's Desk

"You Are Lovingly Welcome to the Sanctuary of Peniel"

Calvary greetings to you in the precious name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ!

At Gaskiya Baptist Church, we believe with all our hearts that when people gather in truth and humility, God's presence becomes real, tangible, and life-changing. Whether you are living within Narayi, Bayan Dutse, Kaduna, or visiting from afar, our doors and hearts are wide open to embrace you.

Our assignment is plain and urgent: to preach the Word in season and out of season, to disciple believers into Christlike maturity, and to provide a sanctuary where burdens are lifted and souls find eternal hope. We invite you and your family to fellowship with us in any of our weekly meetings.
